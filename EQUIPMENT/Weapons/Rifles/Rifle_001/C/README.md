# Procedural rifle visual — machined carbine polish (2026-10-31)

**Source implemented; Godot build/FPS inspection pending.** `RifleMeshBuilder.cs` remains the art owner attached to `Rifle_001.tscn → Visual`. It generates named **Stock, Receiver, ControlsAndMagazine, Handguard, BarrelAndMuzzle, IronSights, SurfaceMarkings** groups once under `Visual/Generated`, then sleeps. They are **static visual assembly nodes**, not per-frame gameplay scripts.

- Uses **BroadChamfer** and **BroadTaperRear** as shared, cached bevel profiles in `EQUIPMENT/Weapons/Shared/WeaponVisualGeometry.cs`. The rifle-only corner clipping was refined to **0.18 / 0.16** (from 0.22 / 0.20), with thinner side edging and less reflective steel trims. Existing `Chamfer` and tapered profiles retain their previous geometry, including MachineGun_001.
- Uses restrained brushed metal with a more readable specular response, satin olive polymer and darker textured-looking grip sections. Small physical plate/serial bars, selector markings and fasteners serve as non-emissive decals, with no imported textures/shaders.
- Rear iron sight: **Saddle → foot → uprights → bridge** is a connected frame seated in the rail; front sight: **barrel clamp → bridge → guards/post** is seated on the barrel. This addresses the unsupported/floating parts in the user's screenshot rather than moving the entire weapon.
- Barrel core, heat shield, exposed sleeve, front collar, muzzle brake, lip and dark bore overlap into a continuous assembly. The barrel and muzzle extend about **0.16m** forward, and the front iron sight is moved forward while staying attached to the barrel sleeve. The authored gameplay `Muzzle` marker is **(0,0.055,-0.968)**, still a sibling of `Visual`; the marker was intentionally moved to match the new artwork, while shooting behaviour and stats are unchanged.
- Additional quiet geometric markings include receiver ID ticks, fore-end index stripes and small stock/collar witness marks. These reuse existing materials rather than adding an image-decal system or glowing accents.
- Reused materials/meshes are allocated statically; no added collision, light, particles, raycasts, process/physics callbacks or imported GLB. Added detail means **more static MeshInstance3D draw objects**, so inspect FPS frame time if the same art is ever duplicated heavily in the world.

Preserved: `FirearmController`, `RifleMaster.tres`, weapon stats, gun slots, ADS, physical sway, shot spread, recoil, reload, visuals/tracers and the pre-spread debug ray. Visual recoil still transforms `Visual` and its new groups, while the real muzzle marker remains separate.
