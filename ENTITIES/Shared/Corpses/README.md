# Shared corpse lifecycle — ZD0–ZD4

ZD0–ZD4 are source implemented, **awaiting local compilation, native physics, visual and performance acceptance**. Existing normal/runner zombie deaths are the only corpse integrations. Player movement has the same optional slowdown receiver, **enabled by default in PlayerMaster**; player death, wildlife death, imported Skeleton3D/physical-bone adapters, detached debris, persistence, corpse loot and navigation are not implemented here.

## Ownership and lifecycle

WorldController creates one independent Corpses root and one CorpseManager, shared in World/FPS. The manager receives the player as distance focus. Zombie death captures its monotonic timestamp before deferred handoff, injury snapshot/pose and original visual hierarchy, then shuts down living AI/hearing/vision/collision. Original meshes/materials and missing-limb visibility transfer unchanged. No reconstruction or second damage path.

CorpseBody owns the lifecycle and optional species-independent IArticulatedCorpsePose contract. ZombieCorpsePose supplies the normal/runner rig; shared physics never searches for zombie names or injury enums. The cheap one-box fall remains available with ArticulatedEnabled=false or a pose adapter without articulation.

## ZD4 — bounded articulated death (2026-10-29)

**Implemented in source; awaiting local build, physics/visual and performance acceptance.** Nearby deaths within the existing active-fall budget now use at most **11 connected rigid bodies**: pelvis, torso, head, two upper/lower arms and two upper/lower legs. Cone-twist joints constrain spine/neck/shoulders/hips; limited hinges constrain elbows/knees. Collision boxes now fit individual segments rather than the whole upright silhouette. No motors or living gait run after death. Missing whole arms/legs allocate neither upper nor lower body segments; existing hidden art and visible stumps remain, with leg stumps following the pelvis. Disabled but present limbs still fall normally.

The existing procedural builders add an identity Elbow pivot before visual batching, preserving forearm/hand placement and living animation. Batches never cross that pivot. Mesh/material resources and seeded appearance remain shared; this may slightly change living draw grouping, so profile rather than assuming it is free. Death captures full joint transforms before Deactivate resets the living pose.

One active corpse callback checks its bounded parts. Defaults: minimum 1.4s, all parts below the existing speed thresholds for 0.6s, maximum 4s; lower articulated damping (linear 0.25 / angular 0.45) gives gravity and joints time to settle. At deadline, supported bodies freeze even if still moving. Support is verified near a torso/pelvis box or two limb boxes using at most 11 rays only at settlement; runaway/unsupported bodies dismiss. A hard time cap bounds cost, not guaranteed perfect relaxation on every surface.

Bodies collide with the static world and other active/settled corpses on **layer 9 (256), mask 257**. Internal body pairs are excluded to avoid overlapping joint shapes fighting each other. Layer 8 remains camera obstruction. Actors, firearms, sight/hearing and cameras still ignore corpse contact.

On settlement, cache the actual physical pose, restore original art pivots into their hierarchy, and remove the temporary joints and rigid bodies. One frozen corpse root retains up to 11 static compound contact boxes at the final segment transforms, allowing later falls to drape over the pose without one oversized upright proxy. Do not call the old relaxation pose afterward. Settled bodies do not wake when hit by another corpse. Their art, missing-limb state and soft slowdown remain; they have no frame/physics callbacks. Footprint bounds are derived from the final segment shapes. All runtime art stays under its original root so hide/shadow diagnostics continue to work.

Distance/budget fallback remains the supported one-box static placement and allocates no articulated bodies. Eight active deaths means at most 88 moving segment bodies and 80 joints, fewer with amputations. Settled contact complexity is higher than the old one-box pile (up to 11 boxes × 96 retained corpses); profile representative piles locally. Aging, retirement and unload stop physics and release runtime nodes; the existing 300s lifetime and 8s sink stay unchanged.

## ZD1 — soft piles

Each settled corpse adds one shallow world-aligned CorpseSlowArea box, built once from its transformed proxy bounds. It monitors living layers 2/3 (mask 6), with no solid collision or process callback. ICorpseSlowable is the opt-in species-neutral contract; CorpseMovementModifiers caches the combined multiplier on enter/exit only. One footprint gives 0.8x desired movement speed; additive overlapping penalties cap at 0.65x, never exponential stacking. No change to gravity, shove displacement, input, perception or MoveAndSlide. Both zombies opt in. PlayerSettings.CorpseSlowdownEnabled is now explicitly **true** in PlayerMaster, so both player views opt into the same 0.8× single-pile and 0.65× overlapping-pile floor as zombies. Turn it off in the master to compare unaffected movement. Runtime toggle changes while already overlapping require leaving/re-entering the footprint.

Exit, sinking, budget retirement, actor exit or scene teardown releases applicable modifiers. Areas hold only bounded current occupants and skip freed bodies on cleanup. No per-frame proximity scans or extra queries. Inactive actors do not simulate; their cached overlap multiplier remains ready for reactivation while still inside. The player is slowed by default while walking through settled corpse footprints. Other species can adopt the same interface later.

## ZD2 — aging and sink

One shared 1-second physics timer runs while any corpse is registered. Lifetime defaults to 300s from death, with at most one timer cadence of start delay. It is independent of visibility. Expiry immediately disables pile contact and clears area slowdown, freezes any still-active fall, then starts an 8s downward visual sink (2.2m). The manager enables its frame callback only while sinking; it animates registered sinking roots, never per-limb geometry/materials. No fade/material duplication. At completion the entire body/area/visual hierarchy is freed and deregistered. Empty registry stops the timer and frame callback. Settled bodies have no integrate/process/physics callbacks; their signal-driven areas still monitor overlaps.

## ZD3 — bounded cost

Manager defaults: 96 retained corpses, 8 simultaneous physical falls, physical-fall distance 45m from the supplied player focus. Count checks occur on spawn, not per frame. Nearby recent deaths get fall simulation while slots remain. Distant or overflow deaths use a deterministic immediate lying pose after one support ray and one clearance overlap on a one-shot physics callback. Already-low bodies keep their lying orientation. Unsupported/blocked fallback placement dismisses rather than freezing in air/walls. No backlog or repeated fallback search. Without a focus (standalone fixture), distance filtering is disabled but count limits remain.

At the retention cap the oldest settled corpse retires immediately, releasing effects before new handoff. If none settled, the oldest entry retires. Pressure retirement deliberately skips the long sink to keep a hard cap, including queued-for-deletion bodies whose contact/simulation is disabled immediately. Unsupported/runaway falls still dismiss. Articulated falls collide with other articulated falls and settled piles; their internal segments exclude one another. The legacy proxy fall retains its original collision policy. No live character hard collider is retained.

**Debug → Print corpse budgets** prints retained/max, active falls/max, settled, sinking, fallback and retirement counts on demand. No debug polling. Global caps bound the registry/timer scan/active sink work; native fall work is capped separately. One-shot fallback physics processing stops immediately. This is world-local ownership and world-unload cleanup, not actor/chunk streaming or persistence; current WS2 streams static buildings only. Future actor streaming must explicitly free/deregister associated bodies, not duplicate them on re-entry.

## Settings and validation

Configuration/CorpseMaster.tres owns fall, pile, TTL/sink and budgets. Settings are duplicated and validated before handoff. Maximum caps are manager policy; per-spawn profiles can customize fall/lifetime/pile tuning without bypassing global budgets. Death timestamp defaults to spawn time only for non-zombie direct callers without one.
