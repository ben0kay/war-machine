# CM5 — zombie standing melee

Source implemented; awaiting local compilation and World/FPS acceptance. CM4 is not required. Both species use ZombieSettings.AttacksEnabled and AttackProfile, authored in ENTITIES/TYPES/Zombies/Configuration/ZombieAttackMaster.tres. Initial tuning: damage 10 physical, range 1.65m, arc 80 degrees, vertical tolerance 0.8m; windup 0.45s, active 0.12s, recovery 0.55s, cooldown 1.5s. Existing armour/critical torso fallback applies, so actual vitality loss follows PH0/PH1. No infection or fake damage for feedback.

ZombieAttack.cs is a separate controller partial. The existing staggered brain attempts attacks only while chasing a valid living target, within reach and facing. Existing physics advances active attacks, stops horizontal movement at commitment and holds its facing through recovery, overlays a two-arm windup/sweep/recovery and returns to the species pose. One contact per attack via MeleeAttack.Advance and HitTargetResolver.Dispatch; target-only filtering prevents accidental friendly damage while other bodies remain cover blockers. The shared optional intendedTarget argument defaults null, preserving player shoves. No player input/camera dependency in the executor. ICombatLifeState is an optional receiver lifetime contract, implemented by PlayerController.

Dodge out of range/arc or move behind cover to make the active-window query miss. The zombie still completes its committed swipe and recovery, even after sight loss; no retargeting or tracking turn occurs. Hit direction is captured at attack start, while query origin follows the solid actor if externally displaced. An accepted bullet/bat/shove reaction, source death/deactivation/unload, invalid/dead target, disabled attacks or loss of standing mobility still cancels pending contacts. Cancel preserves cooldown. Dead players stop being visual chase targets at the next brain decision. Existing HealthComponent handles immunity/mitigation/death latch and HUD notifications; no new player knockback, invulnerability or camera recoil is added. Multiple zombies have independent bounded cooldowns, not a global concurrency budget.

ELD3 remains separate: low-body/immobilized zombies do not attack in this first standing-strike pass. Full missing/disabled-arm eligibility, crawl/bite alternatives and variant attack selection are not implemented. Severed visuals remain hidden; no anatomical or locomotion data is changed. Knife, grapples, infection, navigation, outgoing robot/wildlife attacks and stamina remain out of scope.

Performance: no extra timer/process callback, global scan or persistent Area3D. Idle and distant actors perform no attack physics queries. The existing brain performs a reach/facing check; active windows perform bounded overlaps (32 candidates) and cover rays through the shared executor. Existing settled physics sleeps after recovery and wakes via the brain when cooldown permits. Templates/meshes/shadows remain unchanged.
