# Melee foundation (CM2-A)

The actor-neutral `MeleeAttack` helper and `MeleeSettings` resource are now **wired to Q quick shove** via `PLAYER/Equipment/PlayerQuickMelee.cs` and both camera-mode adapters. `COMBAT/Configuration/QuickShoveMaster.tres` is the initial zero-damage profile.

Create one `MeleeAttack(actorCollisionBody)` per attacking actor when equipment/AI is initialized. Call `TryStart(profile)` on input/AI action; while `IsActive`, call `Advance(delta, bodyOrWeaponOrigin, aimDirection, mode, out contact)` from its existing physics tick. A true result contains the collider, supported actor, closest confirmed contact point and horizontal push direction. CM2-C sends it through `HitTargetResolver.Dispatch`, not a second damage system. Call `Cancel()` on interrupt, death, equipment change or unload; no idle callback is introduced.

**View semantics:** `MeleeAimMode.Horizontal` (top-down) projects facing onto XZ and applies a bounded height tolerance, while `ThreeDimensional` (FPS) checks the whole direction including camera pitch. Origin must be near the character's upper body rather than at the overhead camera. The shape query is a temporary sphere around origin filtered by distance, 65° arc, receiver eligibility and a short occlusion ray against the same collision mask. A wall/door must block the ray. Only one closest eligible recipient can be contacted per attack; the latch prevents duplicate hits during active frames.

**Performance:** Cached query resources; at most 32 physics overlap candidates and one short LOS ray per eligible candidate, only during the bounded active attack. There is no scene search, permanent `Area3D`, new timer or per-frame idle work. Timed cooldown expires without polling.

**Limitations:** Contact point uses the first direct child CollisionShape3D as a centre reference, with the collision body origin as a fallback for other shapes; large wildlife or complex mesh colliders may require more precise closest-point sampling in future. Broad-phase candidates have a fixed cap and aren't pre-sorted; very dense crowds should be tested. Swept multi-target swing arcs and per-target dedup are deferred to CM3. CM2-A itself does not change zombie reactions; CM2-C now binds Q without affecting left-click, while CM2-D animation remains planned.

**Local validation:** `dotnet build war-machine.csproj`; later use a temporary caller to exercise TryStart/Advance in top-down and FPS, check in-range, out-of-range, behind-wall, elevated-floor, aimed-high/low FPS misses, no duplicate contact and cancellation. Q now initiates a shove in both views (FPS requires mouse capture).

## Player momentum scaling — small Q tuning pass

`PlayerQuickMelee.TryStart()` captures the player's XZ movement speed only when a Q attack **successfully begins**. The shared `MeleeAttack` hit query, view-specific horizontal/FPS aiming, separate cooldown and one-target dispatch stay unchanged. The `QuickShoveMaster.tres` exports `StationaryStrengthMultiplier` (0.45), `RunningStrengthMultiplier` (2.25) and `FullMomentumSpeed` (8 m/s). Walking strength lies between the endpoints according to actual speed. At 0/5/8 m/s and base impact strength 1, resolved impact strengths are approximately 0.45/1.575/2.25 before the zombie's resistance. Only Shove impact strength scales, **not damage or stagger duration**. Zombie max knockback speed is now 10 m/s to avoid flattening run-tier force. Everything sleeps while idle; no new animation (CM2-D) or movement action is introduced.
